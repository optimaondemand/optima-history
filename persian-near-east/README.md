# The Persian Near East

Content on the Achaemenid Persian Empire and the ancient Near East under its rule: Cyrus the Great,
Darius, and Xerxes, Persian administration and religion (Zoroastrianism), and the empire's reach from
Anatolia to the Indus. Told from the Persian side rather than the Greek one; the Persian Wars as seen from
Greece belong in `archaic-classical-greece/`. Currently empty — no lesson content ported yet.

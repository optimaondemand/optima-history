# America After 9/11: The Global War on Terror and Populism

American history from the attacks of September 11, 2001 to the present. The folder name points to two
defining threads of the period, the Global War on Terror and the rise of populism, but the scope is the
whole stretch: anything that happens in America from September 11 forward is fair game. Picks up directly
where `america-long-1990s/` leaves off.

A chronological-era folder, not a cross-cutting narrative; see the root README's note on the two kinds. It
is a catch-all for American history within these bounds, in the same spirit as `america-cold-war-era/`.
Because it runs to the present, its end is open, and recent events may be too close to have settled into a
narrative with a clear resolution. As with every folder here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

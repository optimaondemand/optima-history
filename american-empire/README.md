# American Empire

The United States as an imperial power: the Philippines and the other overseas possessions and
protectorates taken or exercised around the turn of the twentieth century, and the arguments for and
against empire that went with them. The story begins when American expansion leaves the continent and
resolves as the country settles into its place as a world power, whatever one calls that settlement.

A cross-cutting narrative folder like `american-west/`, not a chronological-era folder; see the root
README's note on the two kinds. It overlaps in time with `gilded-progressive/`, and a lesson can sit here
even when another folder's timeframe also reaches it. Where the overlap creates a real placement question, a
lesson goes by emphasis: a lesson mainly about empire and America's role abroad belongs here, and a lesson
mainly about domestic politics, business, or reform belongs in `gilded-progressive/`. As with every folder
here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

# The Hellenistic World

The post-Alexander world: the Macedonian conquests and the successor kingdoms (Ptolemaic Egypt, the
Seleucid Empire, Antigonid Macedon), and the spread of Greek language, culture, and philosophy across the
eastern Mediterranean and Near East up to Roman absorption. Split out as its own topic rather than folded
into Ancient Greece, since it is a distinct political and cultural period with its own figures and events.

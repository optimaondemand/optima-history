# The Hellenistic World

Begins with the man who made it possible: Alexander and his conquests. Covers Alexander's campaigns, the
successor kingdoms that followed his death (Ptolemaic Egypt, the Seleucid Empire, Antigonid Macedon), and
the spread of Greek language, culture, and philosophy across the eastern Mediterranean and Near East up to
Roman absorption. The independent polis world before Philip's conquest of Greece belongs in
`archaic-classical-greece/`, not here. Currently empty — no lesson content ported yet.

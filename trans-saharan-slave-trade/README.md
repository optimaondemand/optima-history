# Trans-Saharan Slave Trade

The slave trade carried across the Sahara, from sub-Saharan Africa to North Africa and the wider Islamic
world, running for many centuries before the Atlantic trade began and continuing well after it. The focus is
the trade itself: the caravan routes, the Saharan and Sahelian states and cities that profited from it, the
markets it fed, and the people it moved. The overseas Atlantic trade belongs in `triangle-trade-slavery/`.

A cross-cutting narrative folder like `american-west/`, not a chronological-era folder; see the root
README's note on the two kinds. A lesson can sit here even when another folder's timeframe also reaches it.
As with every folder here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

# Roman Republic

Content on Rome's republican political forms (the Senate, consuls, tribunes, the struggle of the orders),
and major figures and events from the founding of the Republic through the Punic Wars, the late-Republic
crises, and Caesar's rise, up to the Republic's collapse into one-man rule. Split out from the earlier
combined "ancient-rome" folder; the Empire that follows belongs in `roman-empire/`. Currently empty — no
lesson content ported yet.

# Bronze Age and Dark Age Greece

The Aegean world before the Archaic period: the Minoans on Crete, the Mycenaean palace centers and their
record-keeping (Linear B, the Pylos tablets), the Bronze Age collapse as it struck Greece, and the Dark Age that
followed (roughly 1150-800 BC), including oral tradition. The traditional date of the first Olympiad (776 BC) is
this folder's bookend; `archaic-classical-greece/` picks up from there.

**Homer placement:** a lesson whose point is to use the Homeric poems to illuminate the Bronze Age or the Dark Age
(what they preserve of the Mycenaean world, what they show about oral tradition) belongs here. Homer himself was
almost certainly an Archaic-period figure, and the ways the poems functioned as a canon belong in
`archaic-classical-greece/`. As with every folder here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

# American Civil War

Content on the road to the Civil War, the war itself, and Reconstruction: the sectionalism period (the
Missouri Compromise, the Nullification Crisis, the Compromise of 1850, the Kansas-Nebraska Act, Dred
Scott, and the other crises and compromises that deepened the North-South divide), secession, the war's
major campaigns and figures, its outcome, and Reconstruction through its end (1877), which is this
folder's bookend — `gilded-progressive/` picks up from there. This is the default home for Civil War
content — see `american-texas/` for the tiebreaker on lessons that could
plausibly go either place (e.g. John Bell Hood, Texas regiments in the Army of Northern Virginia). A lesson
about the Army of Northern Virginia, the Confederate states generally, or Confederate generals belongs
here; only a lesson with a genuinely heavy Texas-specific focus belongs in `american-texas/` instead. The
Texas History course can draw individual lessons from here if a teacher wants that material, alongside
`spanish-republic-texas/` and `american-texas/`.

# American Civil War

Content on the Civil War itself: secession, the war's major campaigns and figures, and its outcome.
Currently empty — no lesson content ported yet, though this is where a course emphasizing
Annexation-as-a-cause-of-the-Civil-War (per a state whose standards frame it that way) would pull from,
alongside `spanish-republic-texas/` and `american-texas/`. Texas's own secession and Civil War experience
specifically belongs in `american-texas/`; this folder is the war's broader national narrative.

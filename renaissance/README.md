# Renaissance

The Renaissance, with a core running from the fall of Constantinople (1453) to Luther's Ninety-Five Theses
(1517). The years 1357 to 1517 are certainly inside it. The boundaries are fuzzy on purpose: developments in
Italy before the fall of Constantinople, and art after the Reformation, can extend it in acceptable ways.
A cross-cutting narrative folder like `american-west/`, not a chronological-era folder; see the root
README's note on the two kinds. It doesn't exclude other period folders that cover the same years, and a
lesson can sit here even when another folder's timeframe also reaches it.

**Placement note with `reformation-confessional-europe/`:** Baroque art is treated within
`reformation-confessional-europe/`. Art up until the Baroque can easily count as Renaissance and belongs
here. As with every folder here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

# Archaic and Classical Greece

Begins with the traditional date of the first Olympiad (776 BC); what comes before belongs in
`bronze-dark-age-greece/`.

**Homer placement:** Homer himself was almost certainly an Archaic-period figure, and the ways the poems
functioned as a shared canon (education, moral reasoning, cultural identity) belong here. A lesson whose point
is to use the poems to illuminate the Bronze Age or the Dark Age goes in `bronze-dark-age-greece/` instead.

Content on Greek political forms (the poleis, Athenian democracy, Spartan oligarchy), and major figures and
events (the Persian Wars, the Peloponnesian War) up through Philip II's conquest of Greece at Chaeronea —
the end of the independent polis. Greece's place at the head of the Western philosophical and political
tradition belongs here too. Alexander's conquests and everything after belong in `hellenistic-world/`, not
here — Philip is the bookend, Alexander is where the next folder begins.

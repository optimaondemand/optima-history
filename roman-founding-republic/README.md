# Roman Founding and Republic

Content on Rome's founding legends and monarchy (Romulus and Remus, the rape of the Sabine women, the
seven kings including Tarquinius Superbus) through the Republic's political forms (the Senate, consuls,
tribunes, the struggle of the orders), and major figures and events from the Republic's founding through
the Punic Wars, the late-Republic crises, and Caesar's rise, up to the collapse into one-man rule. Renamed
from "roman-republic" to make room for the mythical/regal material that precedes it. The Empire that
follows belongs in `roman-empire/`. Currently empty — no lesson content ported yet.

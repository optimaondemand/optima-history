# High Middle Ages

Content on Europe from roughly 1000 to 1300, the High Middle Ages: the feudal order and the manor, the
medieval Church and the papacy, the Crusades, the growth of towns, trade, and universities, the rise of the
monarchies, and scholasticism. Picks up directly where `early-middle-ages/` leaves off, at the
turn-of-the-millennium boundary.

The dates are centered on Europe and describe Europe's own story arc. The story can extend outward where
it touches Europe (the Crusades and the Islamic world, for example), but other civilizations have their own
eras and timelines, and their 1000-1300 is not necessarily a "high" period or a period at all. As with every
folder here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

# Industrial Revolution

Global, not limited to one country. Scoped by relevance the way `world-war-ii/` is, rather than a fixed
time range: covers the technological and economic transformation itself — Britain's textile mills and the
steam engine, the factory system, the transportation revolution (canals, railroads), and its spread to
Continental Europe and America — wherever a lesson's point is that transformation rather than a particular
country's own domestic story.

Overlaps with `gilded-progressive/`, which already covers post-Civil War American industrialization as
part of America's own Gilded Age story. A lesson told as America's domestic experience (robber barons,
labor conditions, urbanization in the U.S.) stays in `gilded-progressive/`; a lesson whose point is the
Industrial Revolution as a global or trans-Atlantic phenomenon belongs here instead. As with every folder
here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

# Texas History

Content specific to Texas's path to statehood: Austin's colony, the Texas Revolution, the Republic of
Texas, the Texas Rangers and the Comanche frontier, and Annexation. Deliberately kept as its own topic
rather than folded into a generic "American westward expansion" bucket, since it's substantial enough (and
distinct enough) to be its own thing that other states' US History courses can still draw single lessons
from (e.g. a lesson on Annexation, for a course elsewhere that treats Texas as one step toward the Civil
War). Currently empty — no lesson content ported yet.

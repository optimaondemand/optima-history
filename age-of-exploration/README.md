# Age of Exploration

European exploration and colonization, roughly 1400s-1700s: Spanish, French, and English ventures into the
Americas, first contact with Native peoples, and the early colonial period. Includes the entradas that
precede actual Spanish settlement of Texas — Cortes, Cabeza de Vaca, and Coronado — since those belong here
rather than in `spanish-republic-texas/`, which begins later, with the presidios and missions. Currently
empty — no lesson content ported yet.

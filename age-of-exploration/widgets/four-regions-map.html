Critique the Map: Four Texases, Not One Desert
Some real published curricula group Texas with Oklahoma, New Mexico, and Arizona as one "Southwest" region and picture it with a saguaro cactus. Reveal the four real regions, on the real shape of the state, to see what that picture leaves out.
"The Southwest": Texas, Oklahoma, New Mexico, Arizona 🌵
This is what some real published curricula show: one region, one desert, one saguaro cactus as the "typical" landscape.
There are no saguaros in Texas. That cactus grows only in the Sonoran Desert of Arizona and northern Mexico. And Houston averages about 50 inches of rain a year. Seattle averages about 38.

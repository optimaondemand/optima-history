# Ottoman Period in North Africa

North Africa under Ottoman rule or Ottoman suzerainty, beginning with the Ottoman conquest of Egypt in 1517
and the establishment of the regencies of Algiers, Tunis, and Tripoli in the sixteenth century. The story
covers how the provinces were governed and how far Istanbul's control actually reached, and it ends where
Ottoman authority gives way to European colonial rule, which differs by province.

The Barbary corsairs and their captives, and the slave trade across the Sahara, are part of this region's
story, but the trans-Saharan trade has its own home in `trans-saharan-slave-trade/`. As with every folder
here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

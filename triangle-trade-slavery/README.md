# Triangle Trade and Slavery

The overseas slave trade, beginning in 1441 when the Portuguese became involved and ending with its
effective suppression by the British Navy, though slavery itself persisted in many places afterward. The
focus is the slave trade across the sea, not slavery as an institution in every place. After that trade
ends, slavery in Brazil, for example, belongs in a Latin American folder. The long Arab slave trade going
back centuries and the Barbary slave trade also belong in other folders.

A cross-cutting narrative folder like `american-west/`, not a chronological-era folder; see the root
README's note on the two kinds. A lesson can sit here even when another folder's timeframe also reaches it.
As with every folder here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

# Gilded Age and Progressive Era

Picks up where `american-civil-war/` leaves off (Reconstruction's end, 1877): post-Civil War
industrialization and immigration, urbanization, political machines and the robber barons, the labor
movement, and Populism, then the Progressive Era's reform movements (trust-busting, muckrakers, women's
suffrage, direct democracy reforms), running through American entry into World War I, which is this
folder's bookend — `roaring-20s-great-depression/` picks up from there.

A lesson on American industrialization told as part of this era's own story (robber barons, labor
conditions, urbanization in the U.S.) stays here; a lesson whose point is the Industrial Revolution as a
global or trans-Atlantic phenomenon belongs in `industrial-revolution/` instead — see that folder's
README.

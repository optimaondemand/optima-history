# Great Awakenings

The religious revivals of the Great Awakenings. The first, on both sides of the Atlantic, falls in roughly
the 1730s and 1740s and is associated with individuals: the ministries of Jonathan Edwards, John Wesley,
and George Whitefield. The Second Great Awakening is also covered here, and it is associated more with
denominations and movements than with individuals: the rise of Methodism, the Stone-Campbell movement, and
the Holiness and Restorationist movements. A cross-cutting narrative folder like `american-west/`, not a
chronological-era folder; see the root README's note on the two kinds. The boundaries are fuzzy, and a
lesson can sit here even when another folder's timeframe also reaches it. As with every folder here, this
boundary is guidance, not a rule.

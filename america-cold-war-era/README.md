# America in the Cold War Era

The catch-all folder for American history from 1945 to 1991. Anything that happens in America during this
period is fair game here unless a more specific folder covers it better: `civil-rights-counterculture/` for
the upheaval at home, and `cold-war/` for the global rivalry itself. Postwar prosperity and suburbia,
politics and presidencies, the economy, technology and the space program, and everyday life are the kind of
material that lands here by default.

A chronological-era folder, not a cross-cutting narrative; see the root README's note on the two kinds. It
picks up where `roaring-20s-great-depression/` and the war leave off, and it ends with the close of the
Cold War in 1991. Where a placement question arises, the more specific folder wins and this one is the
default. Overlaps with `texas-wwii-postwar/`: a lesson with a genuinely Texas-specific focus belongs there.
As with every folder here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

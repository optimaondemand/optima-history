# World War II

Global, not limited to the American experience. The test for what belongs here isn't a date range but
whether the material is geared toward explaining or telling the war itself — so this folder can reach back
to Weimar Germany and the rise of Nazism, Japanese imperialism and militarism, and the Great Depression as
a global phenomenon, as long as the lesson's point is how that fed into WWII. Covers the war's major
theaters, figures, and turning points, through its end in 1945 and immediate aftermath (the atomic bombs,
Nuremberg, the war's settlement).

A cross-cutting narrative folder like `american-west/`, not a chronological-era folder — see the root
README's note on the two kinds. Overlaps with `roaring-20s-great-depression/`: purely domestic American
1920s/Depression content (not framed around causing the war) stays there; content whose point is
explaining WWII's causes belongs here instead, wherever in the 1920s-30s it starts. As with every folder
here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

# New Spain: Hapsburg and Bourbon

The colonial administration, settlement, and government of New Spain under the Hapsburg and then Bourbon
dynasties, beginning in 1535 with the establishment of the viceroyalty and ending with Mexican
independence in 1821. Exploration plays a smaller role here; it belongs mainly in `age-of-exploration/`.
This folder is about how Spain organized, settled, and governed its American holdings. Where a lesson is
mainly about Texas, it fits better in `spanish-republic-texas/`.

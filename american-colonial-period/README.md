# American Colonial Period

East of the Mississippi (or within its watershed), not the Spanish colonial story — that belongs to
`spanish-republic-texas/` and `age-of-exploration/` instead. Begins with the first English, Dutch, and
French settlements in the region (Jamestown, Plymouth, New Netherland, New France) and runs through
colonial development and rivalry. Ends at the French and Indian War, which is this folder's bookend;
`american-revolutionary-period/` picks up at that war's resolution. Currently empty — no lesson content
ported yet.

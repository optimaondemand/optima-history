# American Early Republic

Washington's inauguration through the War of 1812. Picks up exactly where `american-revolution-founding/`
leaves off. Covers Federalist-era politics (Hamilton vs. Jefferson, the Whiskey Rebellion, the XYZ Affair,
the Alien and Sedition Acts), the Louisiana Purchase, and the War of 1812 itself — the war, and the
political realignment it produced (the Federalist Party's collapse, the Era of Good Feelings), is this
folder's bookend. The Missouri Compromise, which opens the sectionalism content in `american-civil-war/`,
picks up from here. Currently empty — no lesson content ported yet.

📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
A constitution written fast, borrowing from two traditions at once.
The Constitution of 1836 combined a U.S.-style structure with legal habits colonists already knew from the Mexican period. Building a functioning government from nothing, for a population that could not yet pay for any of it, was the Republic's first real test of responsibility.

📚 PART 1 · TWO TRADITIONS IN ONE DOCUMENT
⚡ TRY IT · SORT EACH FEATURE
For each feature of the Constitution of 1836, does it come mainly from the U.S. constitutional tradition or from Mexican legal tradition the colonists already knew?
1. Separation of powers into three branches of government
2. Community property law for married couples
3. The land-grant system inherited from the colonization laws

The new republic kept legal traditions colonists were already accustomed to from the Mexican period, alongside a U.S.-modeled governmental structure. Slavery was written into this constitution explicitly, unlike the workarounds and toleration colonists had relied on under Mexican law.

📚 PART 2 · A TREATY THAT DID NOT HOLD, A RECOGNITION THAT DID

David Burnet led an interim government immediately after San Jacinto, with Lorenzo de Zavala serving as ad interim vice president. Burnet's government negotiated the Treaties of Velasco with a captured Santa Anna. Mexico's own government repudiated the treaties almost immediately, since Santa Anna was a prisoner, not someone Mexico considered authorized to bind the nation to anything.

⚠️ WATCH OUT
Do not confuse a treaty with recognition. A repudiated treaty signed by a captured general is not the same thing as another nation formally recognizing Texas's independence, and Texas still needed the second thing badly.

Houston won the Republic's first presidential election and served its first real term, shaping what the office itself would look like in practice. In March 1837, the United States formally recognized Texas's independence, a real diplomatic win, but Washington refused annexation at that time. Recognition and annexation are not the same question, and only one of them was answered this year.

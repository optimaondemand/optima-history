📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
The hinge. Everything to this point has been prologue.
Moses Austin secured Mexico's first empresario grant in January 1821. Six months later, he was dead, and his son Stephen inherited an obligation he had never sought and had done nothing to create. The continuous political community that becomes the State of Texas begins here, not with a landing or a battle, but with a colony and the man who reluctantly agreed to build it.

📚 PART 1 · AN OBLIGATION HE NEVER SOUGHT

Moses Austin secured his colonization grant in January 1821. Six months later, he died. Stephen F. Austin, his son, inherited the obligation to carry out a project he had not personally negotiated and had not personally wanted, left unfinished only six months after his father secured it. What Stephen does with that inheritance is where the semester's second half actually begins.

📚 PART 2 · YOUR Q1 CAPSTONE
🎯 THE PROMPT
Three hundred years produced three towns. Explain why, using at least three pieces of evidence from the quarter.

This prompt has more than one honest answer, because the quarter's causes are multiple and interacting: the conquest model's dependence on an existing population to inherit (Weeks 2–4), the horse's inversion of the frontier balance (Week 7), the Bourbon Reforms' slow hollowing-out (Week 8), and more. A strong answer draws on at least three of these, not just one.

Full instructions, the rubric, and the submission format are in the separate Q1 Capstone assignment. Read it before you start writing.

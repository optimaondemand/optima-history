# Spanish and Republic Texas

Content on Texas's path to statehood, beginning where actual Spanish settlement begins — the presidios and
missions — not with the earlier entradas. Covers the Spanish-French rivalry over the claim to Texas (La
Salle's incursion and Spain's response), Spanish Texas through Mexican independence, Austin's colony, the
Constitution of 1824 and its failure, the Texas Revolution, the Republic of Texas, the Texas Rangers and
the Comanche frontier, and Annexation. Renamed from "texas-history" to mark its actual period bounds.
Cortes, Cabeza de Vaca, and Coronado — the earlier entradas that precede Spanish settlement of Texas —
belong in `age-of-exploration/`, not here. Deliberately kept as its own topic rather than folded into a
generic "American westward expansion" bucket, since it's substantial enough (and distinct enough) to be
its own thing that other states' US History courses can still draw single lessons from (e.g. a lesson on
Annexation, for a course elsewhere that treats Texas as one step toward the Civil War). Currently empty —
no lesson content ported yet.

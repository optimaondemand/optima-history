# Early Middle Ages

Content on the post-Roman West, roughly 476-1000: the fall of the Western Roman Empire, the barbarian
successor kingdoms, the rise of the Franks and Charlemagne, monasticism and the preservation of learning,
and the Viking Age. Picks up directly where `roman-empire/` leaves off, at the fall of the Western Empire,
and closes at the conventional turn-of-the-millennium boundary, before the High Middle Ages. Currently
empty — no lesson content ported yet.

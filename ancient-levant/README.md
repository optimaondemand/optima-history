# Ancient Levant

The eastern Mediterranean coast and its hinterland (Canaan, Phoenicia, Israel and Judah, and their neighbors)
from earliest settlement through the Persian conquest, which is this folder's bookend: the region passes to
Persia with the fall of Babylon in 539 BC, and the Persian-side story continues in `persian-near-east/`. Covers
early farming sites such as Jericho, the Phoenician city-states and their colonies, and the kingdoms of Israel
and Judah under Assyrian and Babylonian power. As with every folder here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

# Cold War

Global, not limited to the American experience, from 1945 to 1991. The test for what belongs here isn't
only the date range but whether the material is geared toward explaining or telling the Cold War itself,
so this folder covers the conflict wherever it played out, not just in the United States and the Soviet
Union.

A cross-cutting narrative folder like `world-war-ii/`, not a chronological-era folder; see the root
README's note on the two kinds. Overlaps with `world-war-ii/` at the war's end and aftermath: content about
the war itself stays there, while content whose point is the postwar rivalry belongs here. Also overlaps
with `texas-wwii-postwar/`: a lesson with a genuinely Texas-specific focus belongs there. As with every
folder here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

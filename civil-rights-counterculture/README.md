# Civil Rights and Counterculture

The cultural and political upheaval in America from the 1950s through the 1970s. A cross-cutting narrative
folder like `american-west/`, not a chronological-era folder; see the root README's note on the two kinds.
It overlaps in time with `cold-war/`, and a lesson can sit here even when another folder's timeframe also
reaches it. Where the overlap creates a real placement question, a lesson goes by emphasis: a lesson mainly
about the upheaval at home belongs here, and a lesson mainly about the global rivalry belongs in
`cold-war/`. As with every folder here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

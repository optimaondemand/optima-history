# Predynastic and Shang China

China from its earliest farming societies through the Shang dynasty, which is this folder's bookend: the Shang
fall to the Zhou around 1046 BC. Covers the predynastic (Neolithic) cultures of the Yellow River region and the
Shang: oracle bones and the earliest confirmed Chinese writing, Wu Ding and Fu Hao, ritual bronze casting, and
the capital at Anyang. As with every folder here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

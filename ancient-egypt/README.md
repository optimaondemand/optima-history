# Ancient Egypt

Egypt from its earliest kingdoms through the Persian conquest, which is this folder's bookend: Cambyses
conquers Egypt in 525 BC, and the Persian-side story continues in `persian-near-east/`. Covers the Old, Middle,
and New Kingdoms, the Intermediate Periods (including Kushite and Saite rule), and Egypt's relations with its
neighbors, including the Bronze Age collapse as Egyptian records tell it. As with every folder here, this
boundary is guidance, not a rule.

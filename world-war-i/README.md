# World War I

Global, not limited to the American experience, beginning with the July Crisis of 1914 and ending with the
Treaty of Versailles. The test for what belongs here isn't only the date range but whether the material is
geared toward explaining or telling the war itself, so this folder can reach back into the decades before
1914 whenever the lesson's point is how they fed into the war. Covers the war's major theaters, figures,
and turning points.

A cross-cutting narrative folder like `world-war-ii/`, not a chronological-era folder; see the root
README's note on the two kinds. Overlaps with `gilded-progressive/` and `roaring-20s-great-depression/`,
which meet at U.S. entry into the war: purely domestic American content stays there, while content whose
point is explaining the war itself belongs here. As with every folder here, this boundary is guidance, not
a rule.

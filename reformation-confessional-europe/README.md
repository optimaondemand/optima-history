# Reformation and Confessional Europe

The Reformation and the age of religious division in Europe, from 1517, beginning with Martin Luther's
Ninety-Five Theses, to 1648 and the Peace of Westphalia. A cross-cutting narrative folder like
`american-west/`, not a chronological-era folder; see the root README's note on the two kinds. Its dates
mark where the story begins and ends, but it doesn't exclude other period folders that cover the same
years, and a lesson can sit here even when another folder's timeframe also reaches it. As with every folder
here, this boundary is guidance, not a rule.

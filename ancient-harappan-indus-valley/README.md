# Ancient Harappan Indus Valley

The Indus Valley civilization from its early farming roots (Mehrgarh, around 7000 BC) through the Mature Harappan
period (roughly 2600-1900 BC) and its decline around 1900 BC, which is this folder's bookend. Covers the planned
cities of Harappa and Mohenjo-daro, standardized bricks and weights, long-distance trade, the undeciphered Indus
script, and the open questions about how the civilization was governed. The later arrival of Indo-Aryan speech
and culture in South Asia is a separate story and is not part of this folder. As with every folder here, this
boundary is guidance, not a rule.
